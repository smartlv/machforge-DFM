# MachForge

**CNC machining, prototyping, and low-volume production for robotics, UAV, and hardware teams.**

MachForge provides CNC machining, prototyping, and low-volume production support for robotics, UAV, and hardware teams. Based in Shenzhen, China, we support metal and plastic parts from DFM review through production, inspection, finishing, and delivery, working with manufacturing partners in Shenzhen and the Pearl River Delta.

Our focus is practical engineering review, manufacturability, material and tolerance decisions, production follow-up, and quality checks to help identify manufacturing risks before production begins.

## CNC Machining Services

* **CNC milling and turning** — machining for custom metal and plastic components.
* **CNC prototyping** — prototype quantities starting from 1 piece.
* **Low-volume CNC production** — production support for hardware products and small production runs.
* **DFM review** — manufacturability feedback on tool access, tolerances, materials, assembly, finishing, and potential machining risks.
* **Surface finishing** — including anodizing, PVD coating, sandblasting, and electropolishing.
* **Additional processes** — laser cutting and laser marking.
* **Quality control and inspection** — production follow-up and pre-shipment inspection based on project requirements.

## Engineering Review and Production Support

A successful CNC project involves more than machining a drawing. Tool access, tolerances, material selection, assembly requirements, surface finishing, inspection, and delivery can all affect the final part.

MachForge reviews manufacturing considerations before quoting and supports projects through partner manufacturers in Shenzhen and the Pearl River Delta. We help address technical questions, follow production progress, and align finishing and inspection requirements with the project.

**Manufacturing model:** Parts are produced by partner manufacturers selected according to project requirements. MachForge provides the engineering communication and production support from initial review through inspection and delivery.

## Selected DFM Case Studies

* **[316L O-Ring Groove DFM Case](https://www.machforge.xyz/case-studies/316l-o-ring-groove-dfm/)** — a DFM review of four 316L stainless-steel parts that identified an O-ring groove tolerance issue before the first prototype was cut.
* **[6061 Stepped-Bore DFM Case](https://www.machforge.xyz/case-studies/6061-stepped-bores-dfm/)** — a review of a 6061 aluminum holder drawing that raised five points before quoting, including ambiguous 0.25 callouts, Ra 1.6 finishing, and stepped bores.

These cases illustrate how reviewing drawings and manufacturing requirements before machining can help identify questions that need to be resolved early.
